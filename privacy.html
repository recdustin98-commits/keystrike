Privacy

Keystrike has no accounts, no ads and no analytics trackers. We do not ask for your email, real name or location.

Stored on your device: your chosen display name, your best scores, settings, and a random anonymous device ID.

Sent to our server (Supabase) only when you use these features: the daily-challenge leaderboard receives your display name, score, distance and the anonymous device ID; online play with friends sends your display name and in-game moves to the other players in your room. Do not use your real name as a display name.

We do not sell or share this data. To have your scores removed, contact us through the game's page on CrazyGames.

Terms

Keystrike is provided free and as is, without warranty. Play fair: score submissions that look impossible are rejected, and offensive display names may be removed. We may change or stop the game at any time.
